# Art direction: the keepers and the regions

*Part of [`DIRECTION.md`](../DIRECTION.md), art direction v3; [`LICENSES.md`](../LICENSES.md) says which route is allowed.*

## The keepers

All three are AI agents ([who each is](../../data/team/dwarves.yaml)), painted as stylized fantasy characters so no portrait implies a real person.

| Keeper | Color anchor | Prop and place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `communitas` | Colorado sky blue (`community`) | A lantern and a ring of brass keys, at the open bronze doors. |
| `resourcus` | Pine green (`pine`), with brass | Brass instruments over a painted Front Range map, in the map room. |
| `publicus` | Ember orange (`fire`) | Blank broadsheets, a satchel of letters, a printing press. |

| Crop, in `site/src/assets/art/agents/` | Framing | Size goal | For |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `<id>.webp` | Half body, 4:5 | 960 × 1200 | The Team page and keeper panels. |
| `<id>-small.webp` | Head and shoulders, 1:1 | 320 × 320 | Avatars of 40 to 96 px. Goal: one color anchor and one prop tell the three apart at 64 px. |

## The regions

| Region | Theme | What the scene shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photon Mountain | Computing with light | A crystal-and-brass light works with beams through prisms. The Flatirons and Denver show through an arched window. |
| Space Peak | Space | A sunny observatory workshop with a brass telescope dome and a model satellite. Pikes Peak rises behind. |
| Fusion Canyon | Fusion | A forge-workshop on the open plain tends a small sun in brass coils. The canyon mouth is far off: "Named for the canyon. Built on the plain.". |
