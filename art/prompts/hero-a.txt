title: The Great Hall of the Front Range
framing: Wide establishing shot from inside the hall at eye level. Keep the upper left third calm, with open bright sky inside the arch, for a headline.

A grand dwarven forge-hall is carved deep into red sandstone. It has soaring arches, thick timber beams, hammered bronze pillars and long gold and ember banners. Glowing channels of molten gold run along the stone floor behind brass rails, and a great stone hearth blazes at the right side of the hall. In the foreground, small teams of human founders work together at long wooden tables with laptops, sketches and prototypes. A few stout dwarf and gnome keepers carry lanterns and trays nearby. A giant arched opening on the left frames the Flatirons: three huge flat slabs of reddish rock tilted against a pine-covered mountainside. Golden autumn aspens grow below, and the plains stretch east under a clear blue sky. Late-afternoon sun pours through the arch and mixes with lantern and forge light, so the whole hall glows gold and cream.
