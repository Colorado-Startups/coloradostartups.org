A thoughtful stout dwarf scribe and herald has a neat black beard streaked with gray, ink on the fingertips and round spectacles. The dwarf pulls a fresh blank sheet off a large wooden printing press with a satisfied smile. The dwarf wears an ember-red apron over a cream shirt with rolled sleeves and a small gold pin. Stacks of blank pages and ink rollers fill the bright workshop, and sunlight comes through a tall arched window.
