title: The Keeper of the Doors
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A warm, grandfatherly stout dwarf greeter has a long silver-white beard tucked into a wide belt, a bald head and smiling crinkled eyes. The dwarf holds one tall bronze door open with a welcoming sweep of the arm. The dwarf wears a sky-blue tabard with a simple gold mountain-and-sun emblem, a copper-trimmed leather apron and a heavy ring of brass keys. Inside the door there is lantern light and a glimpse of a great carved hall; outside, golden aspens and clear blue sky.
