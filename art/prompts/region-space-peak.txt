title: The Star Works of Space Peak
framing: Wide scene at eye level. Keep a calm, low-detail band across the top quarter for type.

A sunny dwarven observatory workshop is carved into a foothill above Colorado Springs. A great brass telescope dome stands open to the sky, and small brass clockwork cranes lift a gleaming model satellite. Human founders and gnome tinkerers work together at workbenches covered with blank star charts, brass orreries and small rocket prototypes. A stout dwarf keeper polishes a giant lens. Behind them, Pikes Peak rises huge and snow-capped above the city and its red rock formations, under a clear bright blue sky. Warm afternoon sun and lantern light keep everything bright and golden.
