title: The Keeper of the Canvas
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A small, clever gnome mapkeeper has a long white beard, a big round nose and bushy eyebrows. Brass magnifying goggles are pushed up on a tall pointed honey-gold hat. The gnome holds a brass compass over a huge hand-painted map of the Colorado mountains spread on a table. The gnome wears a pine-green coat with many pockets full of pencils, rulers and tiny brass tools. Behind, a cozy map room has wooden map drawers, a ticking brass orrery and a round window showing the Front Range peaks in warm daylight.
