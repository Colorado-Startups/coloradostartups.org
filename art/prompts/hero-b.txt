title: The Doors of the Hall at Golden Hour
framing: Wide shot from the path, slightly low. Keep the upper left third as open, calm, bright sky for a headline.

The great hammered bronze doors of a dwarven hall stand wide open in a carved red sandstone cliff at the foot of the Flatirons. Warm forge light and gold banners spill out of the doorway. A wide stone path through golden autumn aspens leads up to the doors, and human founders with backpacks and laptops walk up it in small friendly groups. One stout bearded dwarf keeper in a sky-blue cloak greets them at the threshold with a lantern raised. Above the doors, three huge tilted slabs of reddish rock rise against a pine-covered mountainside. Far to the left, the long Front Range skyline fades into soft haze. Golden-hour sun lights the cliff and the aspens, and lantern light glows from carved windows in the rock.
