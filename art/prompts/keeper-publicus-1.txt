title: The Herald of the Hall
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A lively stout dwarf herald with a copper-red beard in a single long braid and a jaunty feathered cap holds up a fresh blank printed broadsheet with a proud grin. The dwarf wears an ember-orange herald tabard with gold trim over a cream shirt, and a leather satchel stuffed with rolled letters and blank scrolls. Behind, a sunny print workshop with a wooden printing press, blank pages drying on lines and warm lantern light.
