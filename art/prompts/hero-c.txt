title: Forge Terraces Above the Plains
framing: Panoramic wide shot from a high terrace. Keep the upper left third as calm, bright sky for a headline.

A bright dwarven city of carved stone terraces climbs the foothills of the Colorado Front Range. It has arched windows, bronze domes and small brass clockwork cranes, all glowing with lanterns and warm forge light. On the nearest terrace, human founders work at outdoor workbenches and long tables under gold and sky-blue banners. They build prototypes beside a few stout dwarf and gnome helpers. Below, the plains open east toward a distant city skyline, with golden cottonwoods along a winding creek. Behind the terraces, the long snow-dusted Front Range skyline catches rose-gold alpenglow while the sky stays clear and bright blue. Warm late-afternoon sun bathes everything in honey-gold light.
