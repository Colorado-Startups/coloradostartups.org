title: The Keeper of the Doors
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A cheerful stout dwarf doorkeeper has a broad auburn beard in two thick braids, rosy cheeks and bright kind eyes. The dwarf waves hello with one hand and holds up a glowing brass lantern in the other. The dwarf wears a sky-blue wool cloak over a cream tunic, a wide leather belt with a big ring of brass keys, and sturdy boots. Behind, a pair of tall hammered bronze doors stand open onto golden aspens and the Flatirons in warm afternoon light.
