title: The Sun Forge of Fusion Canyon
framing: Wide scene at eye level. Keep a calm, low-detail band across the top quarter for type.
negative: narrow gorge, canyon walls, cliffs on both sides

A broad, bright dwarven forge-workshop stands on the open grassy plain at the edge of Fort Collins. It has tall arched brass doors, timber roofs and gold banners. Inside its open front, human founders and dwarf and gnome engineers tend a small glowing sun held in a ring of polished brass and copper coils. Its warm light spills across the workbenches and onto the plain. In the distance to the west, the foothills open at the mouth of a canyon where a river flows out onto the plain. Golden cottonwoods line the river, and the Front Range rises beyond. Wide golden afternoon light falls over open farmland under a clear blue sky.
