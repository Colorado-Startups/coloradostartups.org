title: The Keeper of the Doors
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A friendly stout dwarf woman doorkeeper has thick copper-red hair in a crown of braids, freckles and a wide welcoming smile. She holds a glowing lantern on a short pole and a ring of brass keys. She wears a sky-blue hooded cloak with gold trim over a cream shirt and a leather vest. Behind her, the open bronze doors of the Hall frame the Front Range peaks and golden aspens at golden hour.
