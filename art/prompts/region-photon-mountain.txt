title: The Light Works of Photon Mountain
framing: Wide scene at eye level. Keep a calm, low-detail band across the top quarter for type.

A bright crystal-and-brass workshop hall is carved into the foothills above Boulder. Inside, human founders and a few dwarf and gnome helpers build machines that compute with light. Beams of golden light split through glass prisms and lenses on long workbenches. The beams travel along glowing glass threads and gather in polished brass instruments, filling the room with warm rainbows. Through a giant arched window, three huge tilted slabs of reddish rock rise above the town. Foothill towns run south along the range, and the plains stretch toward a distant Denver skyline under a clear blue sky. Late-afternoon sun and lantern light make the whole scene glow.
