title: The Keeper of the Canvas
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A cheerful young gnome tinkerer has a short tidy brown beard, round brass spectacles and a pine-green felt hat. The gnome stands on a rolling library ladder and pins colorful markers onto a giant painted wall map of the Front Range. A small brass clockwork arm on the gnome's shoulder holds a lantern. A tool belt holds compasses, a spyglass and rolled charts. The map room glows with lamplight and late-afternoon sun.
