A close-up of a flat block of warm sandstone fills the whole frame. Fine chisel strokes, tiny pits and soft sand grain cover the surface in an even, all-over pattern. Every part of the frame has the same brightness and the same amount of texture, like a sample swatch. The light is flat and even, with no shadows, no bright spots and no change from top to bottom.
