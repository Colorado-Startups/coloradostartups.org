title: The Herald of the Hall
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A bright, energetic stout dwarf woman herald has dark curly hair tied back with an ember-orange ribbon and a wide grin. She holds up a sealed letter. A small brass clockwork messenger bird perches on her arm, ready to fly. She wears an ember-orange herald coat with gold buttons and a leather satchel of letters. Behind her, a balcony of the Hall hung with plain gold and ember banners overlooks the sunny Front Range.
