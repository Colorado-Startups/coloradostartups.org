title: The Sun Forge of Fusion Canyon, take 2
framing: Medium-wide scene at eye level, looking into the open front of the workshop from close by, so the small sun and the founders fill the middle of the frame. Keep the timber roof and banners as a calm, low-detail band across the top quarter for type.
negative: narrow gorge, canyon walls, cliffs on both sides, empty workshop, closed doors, building seen from far away, tiny figures

A small bright artificial sun glows at the center of the picture, a sphere of white-gold light held in a ring of polished brass and copper coils. It hangs inside an open dwarven forge-workshop, and its warm light fills the whole room. Human founders of mixed ages in casual clothes work at long wooden benches around it with laptops, sketches and small brass prototypes. A few dwarf and gnome engineers tune the coils with long brass tools. The front of the workshop stands wide open between thick timber posts under gold banners, on the open grassy plain at the edge of Fort Collins. Far off to the west, small in the distance, the foothills part at the mouth of a canyon where a river flows out onto the plain. Golden cottonwoods line the river, and the Front Range rises beyond under a clear blue sky.
