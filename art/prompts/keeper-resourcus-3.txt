title: The Keeper of the Canvas
framing: Half-body portrait, centered, facing the viewer, with clear space around the head for a circular crop and a soft, low-detail background.

A bright-eyed gnome woman cartographer has silver hair in a bun under a little pointed pine-green cap, round brass spectacles and a big warm smile. She adjusts a brass astrolabe and a magnifying lens over a large painted map of the Colorado Front Range. She wears a cream blouse, a copper-colored leather apron hung with gears and pencils, and fingerless gloves. Around her, brass clockwork instruments, globes and map tubes catch golden lamplight, with a window view of the mountains.
