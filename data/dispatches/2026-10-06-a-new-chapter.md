Paul Foley is back to lead Colorado Startups as Founder & President, after almost two years away (Oct 6 announcement to the community).

Micah Mador and Lindsey Rohde led Colorado Startups as co-Executive Directors these past few years and did an incredible job. They now run Startup Hikes, Startup Ski Days, Welcome to Boulder and Welcome to Denver through their Startup Community Project at [luma.com/startup-community-project](https://luma.com/startup-community-project).

The focus now is uniting and helping founders. To help get new projects off the ground, email Paul at paul@coloradostartups.org.

The future is bright 🔆
